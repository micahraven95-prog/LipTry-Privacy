Privacy Policy for LipTry

Effective Date: September 30, 2026
LipTry is developed and maintained by BoldT Team.
We respect your privacy and designed LipTry to process your photos and virtual lip try-on features primarily on your device.
This Privacy Policy explains how LipTry accesses, processes, stores, and handles information when you use the application.
1. Information LipTry Accesses
LipTry may access the following information only when required to provide its features:
Photos selected by you
When you choose a reference image or selfie, LipTry accesses only the image that you select through the Android system photo picker or file selection interface.
These images are used to:
- Select a lip color from a reference image.
- Detect lips in a selfie.
- Apply a selected lip color to your photo.
- Generate a virtual lip color preview.
Camera
If you choose the Live Try-On feature, LipTry requests permission to use your device's front-facing camera.
Camera frames are used to detect your lips and display the selected lip color in real time.
LipTry does not record continuous video as part of the Live Try-On feature.
2. On-Device Processing
The current version of LipTry processes reference images, selfies, camera frames, face and lip detection, and lip color rendering locally on your device.
Your photos and camera images are not uploaded to a LipTry-operated server.
LipTry currently does not operate a cloud account or image-processing service.
3. Information We Collect
The current version of LipTry does not require users to create an account.
We do not currently collect or store on our servers:
- Your name.
- Your email address.
- Your photos.
- Your camera recordings.
- Face or lip images.
- Face or lip landmark data.
- Location information.
- Contact information.
- Advertising identifiers.
- Payment information.
The current version of LipTry does not include advertising, analytics, account login, or payment services.
4. Saved Images
LipTry allows you to save edited photos or captured virtual try-on results to your device.
Saving an image is initiated by you.
Saved images remain on your device and are managed through your device's photo or media library.
You can delete saved images at any time using your device's photo application or file management tools.
5. Local App Data
LipTry may store limited information locally on your device, such as:
- Your selected language preference.
- Temporary files required for image processing.
- App settings necessary for the application to function.
This information is not currently transmitted to BoldT Team servers.
You may remove locally stored application data through your device settings or by uninstalling LipTry.
6. Data Sharing
The current version of LipTry does not sell your personal information.
We do not share your selected photos, selfies, or camera frames with advertisers or other third parties through the application.
LipTry uses software libraries required to provide functions such as camera access, image processing, and face/lip detection. These functions are currently performed on the device.
7. Data Retention and Deletion
Because the current version of LipTry does not create user accounts or store user photos or personal information on BoldT Team servers, we do not maintain server-side personal user records for the current version.
Temporary processing data may be stored locally on your device as required for app functionality.
You may remove local LipTry data by clearing the application's data or uninstalling the application.
Images that you manually save to your device must be deleted separately through your device's photo or media library.
8. Security
We minimize privacy risks by processing photos and virtual try-on information locally on the device whenever possible.
LipTry is designed so that the current version does not require uploading your photos or camera frames to a BoldT Team server.
However, no software or electronic storage system can guarantee absolute security.
9. Children's Privacy
LipTry is not specifically designed for children under the age of 13.
The current version does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
If we become aware that personal information from a child has been collected in a future version, we will take appropriate steps to remove it.
10. Future Features
Future versions of LipTry may introduce additional features such as:
- User accounts.
- Online services.
- Advertising.
- In-app purchases.
- Purchase entitlement verification.
- Analytics or other server-based functionality.
If these features change how LipTry collects, uses, stores, or shares information, this Privacy Policy will be updated before or when those features are introduced, as appropriate.
The privacy practices described in this document apply to the current version of LipTry unless otherwise stated.
11. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy when LipTry's features, technology, or legal requirements change.
Any updated Privacy Policy will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
We encourage users to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
