1. Introduction

LipTry is a virtual lip color try-on application developed and maintained by BoldT Team. This Privacy Policy explains how the current version of LipTry accesses, uses, stores, and shares information when you select a color, try it on a photo, or use Live Try-On.

This policy describes LipTry's own data handling. Android, Google Play, your device manufacturer, photo providers, and other services you choose to use have their own privacy practices. Future versions may work differently, as explained below.

2. Information LipTry Accesses

LipTry accesses information needed for the features you choose to use:

  • Selected reference images and selfies: to sample a color, locate the lips, and create a virtual try-on preview.
  • Camera frames: to track the lips and display a color effect during Live Try-On, after you grant camera permission.
  • Face and lip landmarks: temporary points describing facial geometry, used to position the effect on your lips.
  • Local preferences and working data: such as your language choice and temporary image-processing files.

The current app does not request access to your contacts, location, or microphone. It does not require broad access to your photo library.

3. Photos and Reference Images

You choose the reference image or selfie that LipTry can access through the Android Photo Picker, or the Android document picker where the Photo Picker is unavailable. LipTry accesses the image you select rather than scanning your entire photo library.

Reference images are used to sample the color you tap. Selfies are used for local face and lip detection and to render your selected lip color. The app may make a temporary copy of a selected image in its private cache to complete this work. It does not change your original image.

If you select an image from a cloud-backed photo provider, that provider may retrieve it using its own services. This is separate from LipTry uploading an image to a server.

4. Camera and Live Try-On

Live Try-On requests permission to use your front-facing camera. LipTry processes the camera frames on your device to locate your lips, follow their movement, and display the selected lip color.

The current app does not upload camera frames, record continuous video, or record microphone audio. Frames and tracking results are used as temporary working data for the live preview. Using Capture saves a still image only when you choose to capture it.

You can decline or revoke camera permission in Android settings. Live Try-On needs this permission, but you can use Photo Try-On without granting camera access.

5. On-Device Processing

Color sampling, face and lip detection, lip-mask creation, and virtual lip color rendering take place locally on your device. The detection model is included with the app; the current version does not use a third-party cloud image-processing API.

Face and lip landmarks are used to place the visual effect. LipTry does not use them to identify you, authenticate you, or create a face-recognition profile. These landmarks are not sent to BoldT Team or stored in a server-side user database.

Your photos and camera frames are processed locally on your device and are not uploaded to a LipTry-operated server in the current version.

6. Information We Currently Collect

The current app does not require login, create user accounts, or maintain a server-side user database. It does not send personal information or usage analytics to BoldT Team.

LipTry does not request or collect your name, email address, location, contacts, advertising identifiers, or payment information through the app. Photos, camera frames, and face or lip data accessed for try-on remain subject to the local processing described above.

The current version contains no advertising or AdMob SDK, analytics or remote crash-reporting service, payment or billing service, or subscription feature.

If you contact us: when you voluntarily email us, we receive your email address and the information you include in your message. We use that information to respond to your question or privacy request. This correspondence is separate from automatic collection by the app and is handled through our email provider. Please do not send photos or other sensitive information unless needed for your inquiry.

7. Saved Images

Saving a Photo Try-On result or capturing a Live Try-On image is initiated by you. LipTry writes the resulting image to your device's photo library using Android's media storage system, normally in the Pictures/LipTry collection.

You manage these images through your gallery or file manager. Saved images remain after you close or uninstall LipTry until you delete them separately. Your gallery or cloud backup service may synchronize them according to the settings you have chosen for that service; LipTry does not perform that synchronization.

8. Local App Data

LipTry stores limited information in private app storage, including your selected language, a record of whether the app has requested camera permission, and temporary image-processing data. This supports app functionality and is not used for advertising or user tracking.

These preferences and temporary files are not transmitted to a BoldT Team server. Local settings and cache are separate from images you explicitly save to the shared photo library.

9. Data Sharing

BoldT Team does not sell your personal information. The current app does not share your selected images, selfies, camera frames, or face and lip landmarks with advertisers, analytics providers, or other third parties.

LipTry uses software libraries for camera access and local image and face processing. These features operate on the device; including a library does not mean that your images are uploaded to its provider.

Android, Google Play, device services, and any photo or backup provider you use may process information independently under their own policies. If you email us, the email service processes your correspondence to deliver and store the message.

This policy website: this static page contains no analytics, trackers, advertising, third-party scripts, or cookies added by BoldT Team. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process standard web-request information, such as an IP address, to provide and secure its hosting service under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Visiting this page does not give it access to your LipTry photos or camera.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

There is no LipTry account or server-side photo collection to delete in the current version. You control local data as follows:

  • Live working data: camera frames and tracking results are used temporarily in memory for the preview, rather than kept as a continuous recording.
  • Temporary imported images: the app attempts to remove its imported-image cache during normal cleanup. If a session is interrupted, cached imports older than 24 hours are checked for removal on a later app launch. Cleanup therefore is not guaranteed to occur exactly 24 hours after import. You can clear the app's cache in Android settings.
  • Preferences and private app data: clear LipTry's storage/data in Android settings or uninstall the app to remove its local preferences and private files.
  • Saved results and original photos: delete these separately through your gallery or file manager. Clearing app data or uninstalling LipTry does not delete images in the shared photo library. If another service has backed them up, manage those copies with that service.
  • Support correspondence: we retain messages only as needed to respond, resolve an inquiry, and meet applicable legal obligations. You may request deletion by emailing us; any legally required retention will be explained where applicable.

11. Security

LipTry reduces unnecessary exposure by performing try-on processing locally, limiting image access to your selection, using Android's permission controls, and placing working files in private app storage. Saving a result intentionally places it in the shared photo library, where access depends on your device and other apps' permissions.

No app or storage system can guarantee absolute security. Your device security, installed software, permissions, and backup settings also affect the protection of locally stored information. This policy does not guarantee that data can never be processed by your operating system or other services you use.

12. Children's Privacy

LipTry is not specifically directed to children under the age of 13. The current app does not request accounts or collect personal information through an online app service, including from children.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information to BoldT Team, for example by email, please contact us. We will review the concern and take appropriate steps to delete information where required.

13. Future Features

Future versions may introduce user accounts, Google sign-in, backend services, advertising, in-app purchases, a one-time Lifetime Ad-Free purchase, purchase entitlement verification, or analytics. These features are not part of the current version described here.

If new features change how LipTry accesses, collects, uses, stores, or shares information, we will update this policy before or when the new processing begins, provide relevant in-app disclosures, and request any permissions or consent required by applicable rules. The current local-processing design is not a promise that all future versions will use the same data practices.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to LipTry, its data handling, or applicable requirements. The revised policy will appear at this URL with an updated effective date. For material changes, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent where required.
